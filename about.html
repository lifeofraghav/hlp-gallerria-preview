About HLP Gallerria

Mohali's finest lifestyle destination.

More than a shopping mall: Tricity's most desirable address, in the heart of Sector 62, Mohali.

Sector 62 on Himalaya Marg

Designed with intention and built for joy, HLP Gallerria represents the pinnacle of modern commercial architecture and lifestyle experiences in Punjab.

HLP Gallerria is more than a shopping mall. Located in the heart of Sector 62, Mohali, it is a curated space where premium shopping, gourmet dining, world-class entertainment and unforgettable community moments come together under one roof.

Retail, offices, dining, entertainment, a multiplex, kids' play areas and gaming zones: a destination that grows with its people, with unmatched connectivity, abundant parking and valet service.

Vision

Mohali's shining vision of modern living.

To craft Tricity's most desirable lifestyle address, one that seamlessly blends premium shopping, gourmet dining, world-class entertainment and meaningful moments. Every visit should feel effortless, every interaction warm, and every memory lasting, whether it's a family outing, a couple's date, a solo self-care escape or a gathering of friends.

Mission

Excellence and inclusivity, every day.

Delivering sustainable, future-ready spaces that elevate everyday life, foster belonging and inspire joy, so that the best mall in Mohali becomes the natural choice for luxury shopping, family fun and celebrations that matter.
